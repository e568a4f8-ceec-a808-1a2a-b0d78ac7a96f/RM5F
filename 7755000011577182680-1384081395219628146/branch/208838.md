<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Introducing Geekbench 7</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"intro-description"} -->
<p class="intro-description">Geekbench 7 is a cross-platform benchmark that measures your system's performance with the press of a button. How will your mobile device or desktop computer perform when push comes to crunch? How will it compare to the newest devices on the market? Find out today with Geekbench 7.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://www.geekbench.com/img/parkdale/macos-document.png" alt="Geekbench 7 Document Screenshot"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Simple, yet powerful.</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://www.geekbench.com/img/parkdale/icon-cpu-benchmark.svg" alt="CPU Benchmark Icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">CPU Benchmark</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Geekbench 7 measures your processor's single-core and multi-core power, for everything from checking your email to taking a picture to playing music, or all of it at once. Geekbench 7's CPU benchmark measures performance in new application areas including video conferencing, streaming, and game physics, so you'll know how your system handles the tasks you rely on every day.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://www.geekbench.com/img/parkdale/icon-compute-benchmark.svg" alt="Compute Benchmark Icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">GPU Benchmark</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Test your system's potential for GPU compute tasks including machine learning, image processing, and video editing with the GPU Benchmark. Test your GPU's power with support for the OpenCL, Metal, Vulkan, and CUDA APIs. New to Geekbench 7 is an increased focus on Machine Learning, and support for NVIDIA'S CUDA API.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://www.geekbench.com/img/parkdale/icon-globe.svg" alt="Globe Icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Real-World Tests</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Geekbench uses practical, everyday scenarios and datasets to measure performance. Each test is based on tasks found in popular real-world apps and uses realistic data sets, ensuring that your results are relevant and applicable. Geekbench 7's multi-core tests only run multi-threaded when real applications do, so your scores reflect how software actually behaves.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://www.geekbench.com/img/parkdale/icon-cross-platform.svg" alt="Cross-Platform Icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Cross-Platform</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Compare apples and oranges. Or Apples and Samsungs. Designed from the ground-up for cross-platform comparisons, Geekbench 7 allows you to compare system performance across devices, operating systems, and processor architectures. Geekbench 7 supports Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, and Linux.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://www.geekbench.com/img/parkdale/icon-cloud.svg" alt="Geekbench Browser Icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Geekbench Browser</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Upload your results to the&nbsp;<a href="https://browser.geekbench.com/">Geekbench Browser</a>&nbsp;to share them with others, or to let the world know how fast (or slow) your devices can go! You can track all your results in one place by&nbsp;<a href="https://browser.geekbench.com/user/new">creating an account</a>, and find them easily from any of your devices.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image -->
<figure class="wp-block-image"><img src="https://www.geekbench.com/img/parkdale/icon-geekbench-browser.svg" alt="Geekbench Benchmark Charts Icon"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Benchmark Charts</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Verify device performance using the Geekbench Benchmark Charts. Available on the Geekbench Browser, these charts are based on data aggregated from real users in real-world environments. Whether you're considering a new purchase or are curious about a device's capabilities, use these charts to make informed decisions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
